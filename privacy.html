Your data in MAGIVOX

This pilot uses Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic. You choose when to send content for AI processing.

Your account and progress

Firebase Authentication handles sign-in. Career profile details and learning progress are saved in Firestore under your account. MAGIVOX does not store your account password in your profile. Guest progress is stored in this browser and is lost if browser data is cleared.

Chat and resumes

Your resume is opened in your browser and its extracted text is shown for you to edit. When you agree to AI processing and request a review, that text is sent to Google through Firebase AI Logic. Teacher questions include recent conversation, selected course/topic and recent assessment summaries. Chat stays in this tab unless you choose Save chat to account or Save latest reply as note. Saved Teacher chats keep up to 20 recent messages within a 26,000-character limit; individual saved messages and notes are limited to 10,000 characters. You can open or delete saved items in the Teacher library. Download the full conversation before leaving if you need older messages. Help Assistant conversations are not saved to your account.

Voice recordings

Recording starts only after you press Record and allow microphone access. You can listen, download or delete the recording. Transcribe sends the recording to Google through Firebase AI Logic only after you choose the permission box and press Transcribe. MAGIVOX does not save the audio to your account. Recordings are released when you leave the page.

Interview and English practice

Conversation requests send your current answer and previous session turns to Google through Firebase AI Logic. If you transcribe and submit a recording, that audio is also sent for speech feedback. Audio stays in this tab and is not written to Firestore. When Save final feedback is selected, the completed report, scores, mode, role, level, date, corrected sentences and improved self-introduction are saved in your existing interview history (latest 20 attempts). Full transcripts are not saved; you can download them before leaving. You can turn off report saving, download feedback, or use Settings to export/reset your learning history. Speech ratings reflect only submitted recordings; typed answers cannot establish pronunciation, speech fluency or delivery confidence. All ratings are AI practice estimates, not verified assessments, personality judgments or hiring predictions.

Support requests

Submitting Report a Problem, Contact Support or Feature Request stores your user ID, name, email, category, title, description and submission time in Firestore for the team to review. Do not include passwords or secrets. A saved reference confirms storage, not that someone has reviewed the request or will respond within a particular time.

Service processing

The AI provider processes the questions, selected context and recordings you explicitly send. Avoid sharing sensitive information in AI requests. Hosting and provider services may retain technical request metadata under their own policies.

Reminders and jobs

Calendar reminders are exported for you to import into your calendar. Your calendar controls notifications. Saved job tracking is your own record; marking a job as applied does not submit an application. Follow the original job link and check its current details.

Course progress and certificates

Course records store lesson completion, practice attempts, assessment scores, dates and final-exam evidence under your private user document. Issued certificate verification records contain only the certificate ID, learner name, course, completion date, score and stated duration. Anyone with an exact certificate ID can verify those certificate fields. Only an authorized administrator account can list verification records.

Saved learning tools

Your bookmarks, personal notes, flashcards, notification preferences and read or dismissed notification state are stored with your private user data. AI note, flashcard and quiz requests send only the current text and relevant learning context to the configured AI provider. Manual notes and flashcards continue to work without AI.

Career roadmap

Your qualification, degree, specialization, graduation year, stated skills, interests, experience level, preferred location, study-time preference, roadmap plans and mentor-task status remain inside your private user document. They are used to calculate your roadmap and are not copied to the public recruiter portfolio.

Jobs and applications

Your saved jobs, application statuses, notes, recruiter notes, dates, salary entries, preparation checklists and job preparation packs remain inside your private user document. Sample listings are labelled, and MAGIVOX does not publish or submit your applications.

Administration

Accounts with a Firebase administrator custom claim can access the protected administration page, content-registry records, certificate records, support requests and limited account data needed for platform analytics and support. Ordinary users cannot open these administration collections. Changes to deployed course files still require a new application deployment.

Recruiter portfolio

Your portfolio starts private. If you explicitly select Public and save a share slug, MAGIVOX publishes only your approved professional name, target role, about text, evidence-backed skill labels, projects, certificate details and the professional links you entered. Private learning history, failed tests, account details and support requests are excluded. Returning the portfolio to Private removes its public recruiter page.

Control your data

Settings lets you export learning progress and reset learning progress. Saved Teacher chats and notes are separate: open or delete them in the AI Teacher library, and download notes or a restored conversation there. Resetting learning progress does not delete support requests, saved chats, personal notes or flashcards. You can edit your career profile and change an email account password in Settings. Account deletion is not yet available in this pilot. Use Help & Support for questions.
